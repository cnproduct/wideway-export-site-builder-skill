## 4. Multimodal Logistics & 40HQ Container Load Optimization (Расчет загрузки контейнера 40HQ)

Footwear is a **volume-sensitive and cube-sensitive cargo (Cube Out before Weight Out)**.

### Container Capacity Calculations (40HQ Container — 68–70 m³ usable volume):
* **With Individual Branded Shoe Boxes (В индивидуальных коробках)**:
  - Standard shoebox dimensions: $310 \times 190 \times 115\text{ mm}$ ($0.0068\text{ m}^3$).
  - Master export carton (12 pairs/carton): $620 \times 390 \times 360\text{ mm}$ ($0.087\text{ m}^3$, gross weight $\approx 11.5\text{ kg}$).
  - **40HQ Container Payload**: $\approx \mathbf{520 – 550\text{ master cartons}} = \mathbf{6,240 – 6,600\text{ pairs}}$.
  - Total cargo gross weight: $\approx 6,000 – 6,400\text{ kg}$ (well within rail limit of 24,000 kg).
* **Polybag Bulk Packing (Без индивидуальных коробок / в пакетах для маркетплейсов)**:
  - Master export carton (24–30 pairs/carton): $600 \times 400 \times 450\text{ mm}$.
  - **40HQ Container Payload**: $\approx \mathbf{11,000 – 13,000\text{ pairs}}$ (ideal for promotional supermarket distribution).
