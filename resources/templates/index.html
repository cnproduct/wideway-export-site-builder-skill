B2B Sourcing FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions by Footwear Buyers

Key facts on Minimum Order Quantities (MOQ), custom tooling lasts, SATRA physical testing standards, and international social compliance audits.

What is WIDEWAY's Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) for custom footwear OEM/ODM?

For custom OEM private label production, our standard MOQ starts at 500 pairs per colorway across a standard size run (EU 36-45 / US 5-11). For startup footwear incubators or premium capsule collections, low-volume trial runs from 300 pairs can be negotiated depending on upper tooling and outsole mold availability. For existing open-mold white-label models, MOQ is 200 pairs.

How fast can WIDEWAY develop and deliver custom shoe prototypes and samples?

Our in-house 3D CAD modeling studio, automated digital leather cutting tables, and computerized stitching workshops enable complete sample turnaround in 7 to 10 working days upon receiving tech packs or reference samples. Rapid 3D sole last printing takes 48 hours.

What international social compliance and factory audit certifications does WIDEWAY possess?

WIDEWAY's manufacturing facilities are fully audited and certified under amfori BSCI (Social Compliance), Sedex SMETA 4-Pillar (Labor, Health & Safety, Environment, Business Ethics), ISO 9001:2015 (Quality Management Systems), ISO 14001 (Environmental Management), and GRS (Global Recycled Standard for RPET upper materials).

Can WIDEWAY develop custom outsole molds, shoe lasts, and private label branding?

Yes. We offer turnkey ODM/OEM tooling solutions including 2D/3D sole mold engineering, CNC aluminum/steel mold casting (15-20 days turnaround), custom ergonomic shoe last carving, high-frequency embossed logos, woven tongue labels, custom TPU heel clips, and personalized retail shoe boxes with FSC-certified recycled paper.

What physical laboratory testing standards does WIDEWAY conduct before bulk shipment?

All materials and finished footwear undergo 32 rigorous testing procedures in our SATRA-aligned laboratory, including Bally flexing endurance (tested to 50,000 - 100,000 cycles without cracking), Martindale upper abrasion resistance, sole bonding peel adhesion strength (>= 3.5 N/mm), out-sole DIN abrasion loss (<= 150 mm³), yellowing resistance under UV irradiation, and EU REACH / CPSIA chemical compliance.

What are WIDEWAY's standard production lead times for bulk orders?

Standard bulk production lead time is 25 to 35 calendar days following final pre-production sample (PPS) sign-off and receipt of deposit. Repeat replenishment orders for established models can be expedited to 20 to 25 days, leveraging our 15-minute raw material supply chain cluster in Jinjiang.

What incoterms and export ports does WIDEWAY support for global shipments?

We support FOB Xiamen (primary deepwater port, 45 minutes from our factory), FOB Quanzhou, CIF, CFR, and DDP/DDU to customer distribution centers across North America, Europe, Latin America, Southeast Asia, and the Middle East. Full container loads (20GP ~ 3,000-3,500 pairs; 40HQ ~ 7,000-8,500 pairs) and LCL consolidated shipments are supported.

How does WIDEWAY protect proprietary customer designs and brand intellectual property (IP)?

We enforce strict Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDA) prior to receiving tech packs. All custom sole molds and 3D lasts are registered exclusively to the client, securely stored with tamper-proof RFID asset tags, and never shared or exhibited to third parties.

Can WIDEWAY produce sustainable, vegan, and eco-friendly footwear?

Yes. WIDEWAY is certified under the Global Recycled Standard (GRS). We specialize in recycled ocean-bound polyester (RPET) flyknit uppers, bio-based sugarcane EVA foam midsoles, recycled rubber outsoles with up to 30% regrind content, water-based non-toxic adhesives (VOC-free), and biodegradable bamboo lining fibers.

What commercial payment terms does WIDEWAY accept for overseas buyers?

Standard terms for new accounts are 30% T/T deposit upon order confirmation and 70% balance against copy of Bill of Lading (B/L) and final QC inspection report. For long-term corporate partners, we offer 100% Irrevocable Letter of Credit (L/C at sight) or OA 30/60 days backed by Sinosure export credit insurance.
