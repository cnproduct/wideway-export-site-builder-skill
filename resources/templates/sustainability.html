As consumer demand and international import regulations (EU Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence Directive, US SEC Climate Disclosures) rapidly evolve, WIDEWAY GROUP has spearheaded the transition toward circular shoemaking. Circular Material Science

Our 5-Pillar Eco Footwear Material Architecture

Every component engineered from traceable, certified sustainable feedstocks designed to minimize environmental depletion.

♻️

100% GRS Recycled RPET Mesh

Spun from post-consumer recycled PET plastic bottles recovered from coastlines and municipal sorting centers. Each pair of shoes diverts approximately 6–8 plastic bottles from marine ecosystems.

Global Recycled Standard (GRS 4.0) Verified
🌾

Bio-Based Sugarcane EVA Foams

Renewable sugarcane ethanol synthesized into high-resilience EVA granules. Captures CO2 during crop growth, replacing up to 40% petrochemical content with identical rebound cushioning.

ASTM D6866 Bio-Carbon Tested & Certified
🌿

Algae Bloom Midsole Technology

Harvested from eutrophic freshwater waterways prone to toxic algae overgrowth. Cleaning freshwater systems while producing flexible, odor-resistant, shock-absorbing outsoles and recovery footbeds.

Freshwater Habitat Restoration Proven
💧

Solvent-Free Waterborne PU

Water-based polyurethane adhesives and vegan microfiber leathers engineered without DMF, benzene, or toluene. Delivers zero chemical smell in shoe boxes and clean indoor factory air.

Zero VOC / EU REACH SVHC Compliant
🔄

Recycled Rubber Crumb Outsoles

Up to 30% post-industrial rubber trim waste and end-of-life footwear soles reground into high-traction rubber pods, eliminating landfill disposal while maintaining DIN abrasion resistance ≤ 130mm³.

Closed-Loop Factory Regrind Process
📦

FSC Certified Unbleached Packaging

100% recycled unbleached kraft paper shoe boxes printed with non-toxic vegetable soy inks. Shipped with biodegradable cassava starch polybags or organic cotton drawstring bags.

FSC-STD-40-004 Chain of Custody
