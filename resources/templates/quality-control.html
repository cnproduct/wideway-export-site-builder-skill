Zero-Defect Methodology

Our 5-Gate Quality Assurance Inspection Pipeline

Independent QA inspectors monitor every workstation across raw material delivery, CNC cutting, automated stitching, lasting assembly, and container loading.

GATE 1

IQC: Incoming Raw Materials

Every batch of incoming Flyknit mesh, TPU sheets, action leather, EVA pellets, and rubber soles undergoes rigorous bench testing before warehouse release:

  • Textile color fastness to wet/dry crocking (ISO 105-X12 ≥ Grade 4)
  • Upper tear resistance & tensile elongation verification
  • EVA midsole rebound resilience test & Shore C hardness check
  • Zero phthalates & toxic azo dyes certification audit
GATE 2

IPQC: CNC Cutting & Skiving

Multi-ply oscillating knife cutting beds cut shoe components with CAD digital nesting accuracy:

  • Dimensional tolerance strictly held within ±0.5 mm per piece
  • Bevel edge skiving thickness measured with digital micrometer
  • Fabric grain alignment check across left and right shoe pairs
  • Automated thermal stamping and piece numbering verification
GATE 3

IPQC: Computerized Stitching

Uppers stitched on CNC automated pattern machines and post-bed machines inspected in-line:

  • Uniform stitch pitch (8 to 10 stitches per inch) with zero thread drop
  • Lace eyelet and webbing loop pull-out force test (≥ 180 N)
  • Hot-melt TPU film overlay temperature and bond check
  • Anatomical collar foam alignment and tongue centering check
GATE 4

IPQC: Lasting & Sole Bonding

Computerized hydraulic lasting and conveyor assembly line strictly governed by temperature and pressure curves:

  • Shoe last toe-spring curvature and heel centerline optical alignment
  • Infrared adhesive activation oven monitored continuously (55°C–65°C)
  • 6-Bar hydraulic 3D wrap-around sole pressing for 12–15 seconds
  • Sole peel adhesion destructive test every 500 pairs (≥ 3.5 N/mm)
GATE 5

FQC: 100% Needle Detector & Finish

Every finished shoe must clear complete cosmetic cleaning and electromagnetic security screening:

  • 100% Conveyor metal needle detector scan (Calibrated to 0.8mm Fe)
  • Thermal air de-creasing, glue stain wiping, and clean trim finish
  • Left/right symmetry check, size label alignment, and barcode scan
  • High-strength export carton strapping and silica gel desiccant loading
OQC AUDIT

OQC: Pre-Shipment AQL 2.5

Independent QA team or buyer third-party inspectors (SGS, BV, Intertek, QIMA) conduct random audits:

  • Conforming to ISO 2859-1 / ANSI ASQ Z1.4 Level II sampling
  • Critical Defects: 0 accepted | Major Defects: AQL 1.5 | Minor: AQL 2.5
  • Drop test of master cartons from 76cm on all 6 sides and corners
  • Full seal documentation signed before container departure

Jinjiang Footwear Capital Advantage

Why Sourcing from Chendai Guarantees Rapid QA Turnaround

ChenDai Town in Jinjiang City is internationally renowned as the world's most concentrated footwear supply chain cluster. Within a 15-minute radius of WIDEWAY's facility, over 3,000 specialized material suppliers, outsole mold tooling workshops, and official municipal testing institutes operate symbiotically.

If any physical test or color fastness parameter deviates during incoming material inspection, replacement compound batches and re-engineered Flyknit yarns can be delivered and re-tested within 4 to 8 hours. This hyper-dense industrial proximity eliminates weeks of transit delay experienced by isolated overseas factories.
