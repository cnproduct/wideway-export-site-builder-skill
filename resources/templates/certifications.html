Sedex Members Ethical Trade Audit covering Labour Standards, Health & Safety, Environment, and Business Ethics, facilitating seamless supplier pre-qualification for UK, European, and US retail chains.

ISO 9001:2015 QMS

Internationally certified Quality Management System ensuring complete raw material batch traceability, standardized cutting tolerances (±0.15mm), and closed-loop corrective action protocols.

Visit iso.org Official ↗

SATRA Technology (UK)

The world's leading footwear research and testing authority. WIDEWAY's in-house laboratory adheres to SATRA TM55 (Bally flex), TM31 (Martindale abrasion), TM174 (sole abrasion), and TM411 (peel strength).

Visit satra.com Official ↗

SGS Lab Verified

Independent accredited testing verifying physical wear performance, outsole slip resistance (SATRA TM144 / EN ISO 13287), wet/dry color fastness, and accelerated aging stability for European shipments.

Visit sgs.com Official ↗

Intertek Total Quality

Accredited third-party verification for international export compliance, physical durability testing, and supply chain quality assurance for North American and European brand partners.

Visit intertek.com Official ↗

CE Marking (EU Directive)

European Union conformity verifying that our performance trail shoes, outdoor boots, and athletic footwear comply with EU health, consumer safety, and environmental protection regulations.

Visit European Commission CE ↗

TÜV Rheinland Tested

Independent German technical inspection validating footwear biomechanics, high-impact sole cushioning resilience, and non-hazardous chemical compliance for tier-1 German retail groups.

Visit tuv.com Official ↗

OEKO-TEX ® Standard 100

Guarantees that all textile yarns, mesh linings, foam insoles, and laces contacting consumer skin are completely free from over 300 harmful chemicals, allergens, and regulated substances.

Visit oeko-tex.com Official ↗

GRS (Recycled Standard)

Full chain of custody tracking for recycled materials, validating that our eco-friendly footwear uppers incorporate genuine post-consumer RPET recycled polyester fibers and reclaimed rubber.

Visit Textile Exchange GRS ↗

FSC Certified Packaging

Forest Stewardship Council certified sustainable corrugated master cartons, private label shoe boxes, and wrapping tissue sourced exclusively from responsibly managed, deforestation-free forests.

Visit fsc.org Official ↗

US FDA & CPSIA Compliant

Workplace Ethics & ESG

Industrial Area Fire Safety Facility & ESG Protocols

Social compliance and occupational safety are integral to WIDEWAY's long-standing OEM contracts with tier-1 brands (FILA, Diadora, Lidl). In full alignment with ISO 45001 workplace health standards and amfori BSCI, our Chendai facility maintains top-tier occupational safeguards.

✓ 24/7 Automated Sprinkler Network
✓ Explosion-Proof Adhesive Storage
✓ Non-Toxic Organic VOC Scrubbers
✓ Quarterly Fire Evacuation Drills
