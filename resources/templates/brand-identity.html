01

Scenario A: Footwear Product & Retail Packaging VI

Tongue Labels, TPU Injection Heel Stabilizers, FSC Shoeboxes & Monogram Tissue Paper
SATRA TM133 Compliant

Physical Engineering Specifications:

  • Tongue Woven Label: 32mm × 45mm high-density damask woven polyester. WIDEWAY icon rendered in Pantone 186 C yarn with 0.15mm edge sharpness; ultrasonic soft-cut edges eliminate tongue friction.
  • Heel TPU Molded Cup: Shore 75A-85A high-rebound thermoplastic polyurethane. Debossed chevron geometry with high-gloss Pantone 186 C red finish tested to 50,000 cold flexing cycles at -10°C.
  • FSC-Certified Shoebox: 350g recycled kraft E-flute corrugated cardboard. 100% natural soy-based inks. Dark navy flood coat with hot-foil stamped WIDEWAY Red top crest and GS1-128 standard barcode labels.
  • Monogram Interleaving Tissue: 17g semi-translucent acid-free paper printed with 30% opacity water-based red repeat logo pattern to guard upper leather against moisture during maritime shipping.

05

Scenario E: International Co-branding & OEM Symbiosis

Dual-Branding Guidelines for FILA, DIADORA, KAPPA, WRANGLER & European Private Labels
Global IP Protection

Co-Branding Governance Rules:

  • 0.5X Protective Clear Space: No client logo, secondary graphic, stitching line, or die-cut edge may intrude within 0.5X height of the WIDEWAY trademark.
  • Optical Weight Hierarchy: Client brand logo maintains 100% optical prominence on outer lateral shoe quarter; WIDEWAY manufacturing endorsement is placed at 60% optical scale on inner tongue label or sockliner sub-stamp.
  • Chromatic Coexistence: When client brand uses competing bright colors, WIDEWAY Red switches to monochromatic high-density black, optic white, or transparent blind deboss.
  • Strict NDA Confidentiality: Proprietary client outsole molds and shoe lasts receive RFID serialization with zero public exhibition without prior written client authorization.
⭐ Real-World Production Case Study
10+ Year Strategic Manufacturing Partnership with FILA & DIADORA

Respecting brand identity boundaries while upholding factory-floor consistency has made WIDEWAY a preferred tier-1 vendor for international brand groups for over a decade, manufacturing over 8,000,000 pairs annually with zero IP disputes.
