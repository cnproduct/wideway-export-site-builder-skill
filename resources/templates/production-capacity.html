Assembly Automation

Automated Assembly Lines & Infrared Activation Tunnels

WIDEWAY operates 4 modern continuous conveyor assembly lines specializing in cold-cemented athletic running shoes, lightweight sneakers, lifestyle vulcanized footwear, and molded EVA recovery slides. Each line features a computerized infrared heat activation tunnel maintaining a constant 55°C–65°C heating curve.

Dual automated sole-pressing stations apply 0.4–0.6 MPa uniform hydraulic clamping force across toe, arch, and heel chambers, eliminating micro-gaps and guaranteeing a sole bonding peel strength exceeding 3.5 N/mm (far surpassing the 2.5 N/mm EN ISO athletic footwear requirement).

Precision Lasting

Computerized Toe Lasting & Automatic Heel Seating

Accurate shoe fit and symmetrical toe curvature define brand reputation. WIDEWAY employs computerized 9-pincer hydraulic automatic toe lasting machines equipped with electronic position memories storing over 120 customer last profiles.

Integrated micro-jet hot melt applicators inject solvent-free thermoplastic adhesive directly into the insole-to-upper interface before the Teflon wiper plates close. Followed immediately by automatic pneumatic heel seat lasting, our process ensures 100% heel counter stability and zero vamp asymmetry across all EU/US sizes.

✓ 9-Pincer Multi-Axis Grippers
✓ Micro-Jet Hot Melt Injection
✓ Pneumatic Heel Seat Banding
✓ Stored Profiles for US/EU/Asia Lasts

Stitching Excellence

Automated CNC Pattern Stitching & Upper Sewing Workshop

Our stitching floor houses 12 high-precision CNC programmable sewing machines and over 40 computerized cylinder-bed and post-bed sewing stations. Programmable optical sensors automatically trace CAD-designed stitch paths with sub-millimeter needle precision.

This automated setup guarantees identical stitch density (8–10 stitches/inch), reinforced bar-tacks on eyestays, and flawless reinforcement on lightweight Flyknit, engineered jacquard mesh, and genuine/synthetic leather uppers, completely eliminating skipped stitches and uneven seam allowances.

Safety & Quality Gates

100% Pair-by-Pair Inspection & Conveyor Needle Detection

Prior to boxing, 100% of manufactured shoes pass through our dedicated finishing and inspection lines. Technicians inspect for aesthetic symmetry, color consistency, cement overflow, and thread ends using industrial hot-air irons.

Crucially for international retailers (FILA, Diadora, Lidl, Aldi), every single shoe passes through a calibrated dual-sensor digital needle detector (sensitive to 0.8mm ferrous metals). Any product with broken needle fragments is automatically ejected and quarantined, guaranteeing zero legal liability for brand importers.

✓ 0.8mm Ferrous Needle Scanner
✓ In-House Bally & Martindale Lab

Quality Engineering

The 8-Stage WIDEWAY Production Process

From incoming certified raw materials to sealed shipping containers, every step is rigorously governed by ISO 9001 and SATRA quality protocols.

01

IQC Material Testing

Incoming mesh rolls, synthetic leathers, EVA pellets, and adhesives undergo physical tensile, colorfastness, and chemical RSL audits before batch release.

02

CNC Pattern Cutting

Multi-ply oscillating knife tables cut precision upper components with CAD nesting algorithms. Laser edges are heat-sealed against fraying.

03

CNC Sewing & Welding

Upper components are stitched on automated template machines. High-frequency TPU weld overlays and reflective badges are applied.

04

Computerized Lasting

Uppers are pulled over precision customer lasts via 9-pincer hydraulic toe lasting and pneumatic heel seat machines with hot-melt glue injection.

05

Roughing & Primer

Sole feather lines are precision-roughed to remove surface shine, followed by specialized waterborne primers to maximize bonding chemical affinity.

06

IR Activation & Pressing

Uppers and outsoles traverse the infrared heat activation tunnel (60°C) before entering 3-stage pneumatic universal presses at 0.5 MPa.

07

Chilling & De-Lasting

Assembled footwear passes through a sub-zero cooling tunnel (-5°C) to freeze adhesive polymers and permanently set the shoe's ergonomic shape.

08

Needle Scan & Pack

100% inline 0.8mm needle detection sweep, insoles inserted, tissue wrapped, packed into brand shoe boxes, and barcode master carton sealed.
