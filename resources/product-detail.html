Detailed B2B specifications, materials, and OEM/ODM private label options for WIDEWAY sports shoes and sneakers.

WIDEWAY operates as an industrial footwear manufacturer in Jinjiang, Fujian, specializing in athletic sneakers, running footwear, casual vulcanized trainers, and molded recovery slides with an annual production capacity of 5000000 pcs across our 25000 m facility. Every production batch undergoes ISO 9001 certified manufacturing quality controls, SATRA TM144 slip resistance evaluations, and in-line tensile testing exceeding 3.5 MPa bond strength. We provide low minimum order quantities starting at 500 pcs per colorway with 7 to 10 days rapid physical prototyping and 30 to 45 days mass production turnaround. Our export logistics division handles international shipments including 1 20GP and 1 40HQ container loads with automated customs documentation for North America, the European Union, and Central Asia under CE, ASTM F2413, and GOST technical compliance frameworks.

Engineering Excellence in Every Stitch

Every shoe produced by Jinjiang Wideway incorporates decades of shoemaking know-how from Jinjiang's world-renowned supply cluster. From computer-aided shoe last digitization to robotic outsole vulcanization and cement bonding, our facility maintains peak athletic performance benchmarks.

  • Dynamic Upper Weaving: 3D flying-knit engineered jacquard mesh with targeted flex zones and reinforced synthetic eyestays.
  • Supercritical Cushioning: Dual-density lightweight EVA foam delivering over 65% energy rebound with reduced foot fatigue.
  • Slip-Resistant Grip: High-abrasion thermal rubber (RB) with multidirectional lug geometry certified to SATRA TM144 slip resistance standards.

📊 Laboratory Test Thresholds

• Bally Flexing Resistance: >100,000 Cycles without upper cracking

• Outsole Peel Adhesion Strength: >3.5 N/mm bond strength

• Martindale Abrasion: >25,600 rubs dry / >12,800 rubs wet

• Colorfastness to Rubbing: Grade 4–5 (ISO 105-X12 standard)
